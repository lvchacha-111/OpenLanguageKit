    03 / CUE CARDS

    Eight parts of the story

    每段有 1–1.5 分钟目标。提词卡是句框，不是完整讲稿；每段再补 2–4 句简单英语或一张图片，讲清一个细节。

    04 / A1 QUESTION PRACTICE

    What might a friend ask?

    找家长或朋友当听众。回答卡住时，可以先请对方重复问题。

    Who is your favourite player?My favourite player is …
    Why do you like this player?I like him / her because …
    What is one thing you learn from this player?I learn to …
    Could you say that again, please?A useful A1 phrase when you did not hear the question.
